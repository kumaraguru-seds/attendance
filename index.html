Retiring the Attendance System

I want to sincerely apologize to every member who was affected or felt uncomfortable with the system at attendance.kumaraguruseds.space.

I initially implemented this system believing it would make our teams more active and workable. I now realize that this approach was unnecessary and did not work as I had hoped.

I take full responsibility for the friction this caused. Your comfort and passion for the club are what truly matter. We are moving forward without this system to focus on a more positive and supportive environment.
